    Module T6 · day 2 · 40 min · Verifiable immunization records across borders: the technical track

    The journey, verifying a card, sharing a link

    When: Day 2 · 11:00–11:20 · Technical track; Day 2 · 11:20–11:40 · Technical track · The two days

    How does an app go from a scanned QR to a verified result, and from a shared link to the records behind it?

    Day 2. Serves the policy module M6 Doing it together.

    Sessions

    • 11:00–11:20 · Recap · Recap: the whole journey and where you fit (20 min). The journey from a birth abroad to a clinic back home to the family travelling again, and the four apps you will walk through in your small group. Leads: Lead facilitator; supports: Trust facilitator.
    • 11:20–11:40 · Talk · Talk 5: Verifying a card and sharing a link (20 min). The trust ideas from Day 1 in practice. How an app reads a vaccination QR and checks it against the trust list, and how a shared health link is opened. Leads: Trust facilitator.

    Where it meets the journey

    • J3a: Scan, Base45 and zlib decode, COSE signature check against the trust list, then import with provenance.
    • J4.0b: The portal encrypts the IPS and DVCs, publishes a manifest and signs the link as an HCERT.
    • J4.1: The viewer fetches the manifest with an HTTP Message Signature, sends the passcode, and decrypts the documents.

    К концу участник сможет

    • Follow a card from scan to verified result
    • Follow a health link from QR to records
    • Say where each of the four apps fits in the journey
