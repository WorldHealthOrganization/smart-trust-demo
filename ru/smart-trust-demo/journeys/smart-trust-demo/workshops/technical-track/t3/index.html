    Module T3 · day 1 · 20 min · Verifiable immunization records across borders: the technical track

    Trust between countries: SMART Trust and the GDHCN

    How can a clinic in one country trust a vaccination record from another, and when is a card better than a link?

    Day 1. Serves the policy module M4 Trust, credentials and wallets.

    Sessions

    • 13:30–13:50 · Talk · Talk 2: Trust between countries, SMART Trust and the GDHCN (20 min). How a clinic in one country can trust a vaccination record from another. Covers the GDHCN network, digital vaccine certificates (DVC) and verifiable health links (VHL), and when to use a card and when to use a link. Leads: Trust facilitator.

    Where it meets the journey

    • J1: Ключ подписанта документов страны Taifafeki (DSC участника XXR) подписывает каждый DVC как CWT в COSE.
    • J3a: The clinic verifies each card offline against its cached copy of the GDHCN trust list, by kid.
    • J4.1: Программа просмотра проверяет HCERT в VHL по DSC страны Onesa (XXU), прежде чем что-либо загружать.

    К концу участник сможет

    • Explain what the GDHCN is and why a verifier needs a trust list
    • Tell a DVC from a VHL and say when each one fits
    • Know which parts you will work with on Day 2
