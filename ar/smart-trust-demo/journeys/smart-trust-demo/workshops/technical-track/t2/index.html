    Module T2 · day 1 · 50 min · Verifiable immunization records across borders: the technical track

    Offline data capture

    How does a form on a health worker's phone become a patient record, with no signal?

    Day 1. Serves the policy module M3 Standards, specifications and the core data set.

    Sessions

    • 11:40–12:30 · Lab · Lab 1: Offline data capture (50 min). The stack is already running on Heropa. Open a questionnaire in the form editor, change it, and watch the change appear in the OHS Player in your browser. Then add a validation rule or a skip rule. If time allows, see how the answers turn into FHIR resources. Optional: a QR code for the gallery app, to try the same forms on iOS or Android. Leads: Lab facilitator; supports: Co-facilitator.

    Where it meets the journey

    • J2a: The CHW app decodes the paper cards (read-unverified) and fills the form from them, without checking signatures.
    • J2b: The household registration Questionnaire; answers become Patient, Group (household) and reported Immunization, queued offline.

    في النهاية، يستطيع المشارك أن

    • Edit a FHIR Questionnaire and see the result straight away
    • Add a validation or skip rule
    • Describe how captured answers become patient records

    الأسئلة، على امتداد الرحلة

    1. J2a
      عامل صحي يعثر على الرضيع — ويقرأ بطاقتيه

      Which data elements can the form take from a scanned card, and how does it mark them as reported, not verified?

    2. J2b
      سُجِّل في المنزل، وأُحيل

      Which validation or skip rule stops a duplicate child being registered in the same household?
