    The technical track of a general two-day workshop on verifiable immunization records across borders, taught from the journeys J1 to J4.1 of one cross-border family. It runs beside the [policy-maker track](../dpi-h-policy-track/) (M1 to M7): each technical module serves the policy module it is paired with, and both tracks meet in joint sessions each morning and at the close of each day. Two days, one cross-border immunization story. Day 1 covers the building blocks and gets everyone running on Heropa. Day 2 is teams building one part of the journey, then seeing it all work together. ## The two days A suggested timetable: a host adapts the times, and fills each country presentation with the host country or an invited country. ### Day 1 Concepts and the OHS stack.
