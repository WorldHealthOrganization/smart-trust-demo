    Module T6 · day 2 · 30 min · Verifiable immunization records across borders: the technical track

    Verifying a card, sharing a link

    How does an app go from a scanned QR to a verified result, and from a shared link to the records behind it?

    Day 2. Serves the policy module M6 Doing it together.

    Sessions

    • 11:00–11:30 · Talk · Talk 5: Verifying a card, sharing a link, and what you build today (30 min). The trust ideas from Day 1 in practice. How an app reads a vaccination QR and checks it against the trust list, and how a shared health link is opened. Then a look at the four pieces the teams will build. Leads: Trust facilitator.

    Where it meets the journey

    • J3a: Scan, Base45 and zlib decode, COSE signature check against the trust list, then import with provenance.
    • J4.0b: The portal encrypts the IPS and DVCs, publishes a manifest and signs the link as an HCERT.
    • J4.1: The viewer fetches the manifest with an HTTP Message Signature, sends the passcode, and decrypts the documents.

    Al final, cada participante podrá

    • Follow a card from scan to verified result
    • Follow a health link from QR to records
    • Pick the team you will join
