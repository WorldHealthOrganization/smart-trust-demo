    Module T7 · day 2 · 195 min · Verifiable immunization records across borders: the technical track

    Team build, showcase and the journey together

    Can each team change one app in the journey and then run the whole journey live, from a birth abroad to a clinic back home to the family travelling again?

    Day 2. Serves the policy module M7 First move and the investment required.

    Sessions

    • 11:30–12:30 · Team build · Team build, part 1 (60 min). Each team takes one app in the journey. Everything is pre-configured, so there is no Android Studio. You run the app, read how its configuration works, and change it. Test with the vaccination cards. Leads: Team facilitators; supports: One per team.
    • 13:30–14:25 · Team build · Team build, part 2 (55 min). Finish the change, test it with the cards, and get ready to show it. Leads: Team facilitators; supports: One per team.
    • 14:25–15:00 · Showcase · Team presentations (35 min). Each team shows what it built, about seven minutes each. Volunteers present. Leads: Lead facilitator (chair).
    • 15:30–16:15 · Team build · Putting the journey together (45 min). Volunteers run the whole journey on the demo system, each using their own piece. This is the rehearsal for the finale. Leads: Team facilitators; supports: A volunteer from each team.

    Where it meets the journey

    • J2b: CHW app team: the registration form, the household, the referral.
    • J3a: Clinic app team: the verifier and its trust store.
    • J4.0a: Family app team: one phone, one profile and one link per member.
    • J4.1: Viewer app team: open the link, enter the passcode, read the records.

    The four teams

    • CHW app (The health worker's phone): Scan a vaccination card, add the child to the household, and refer them to a clinic. Steps J2a, J2b.
    • Clinic app (The nurse's tablet): Receive the referral, verify the card against the trust list, and plan the next visit. Steps J3a, J3b.
    • Family app (The parent's phone): Hold the family's records and share a child's record as a health link. Steps J4.0a, J4.0b.
    • Viewer app (The clinic abroad): Open a shared health link and read the records behind it. Steps J4.1.

    结束时，参与者将能够

    • Run your app and find where its configuration lives
    • Make one change and see it work
    • Have a working piece you can show in under seven minutes
