    Module T4 · day 1 · 65 min · Verifiable immunization records across borders: the technical track

    Workflows, sync and who sees what

    When: Day 1 · 13:45–13:55 · Technical track; Day 1 · 13:55–14:05 · Technical track; Day 1 · 14:05–14:50 · Technical track · The two days

    What should happen next for this child, how does the record reach the server, and who is allowed to see it?

    Day 1. Serves the policy module M5 Governance, decision rights and the legal basis.

    Sessions

    • 13:45–13:55 · Talk · The FHIR Information Gateway (10 min). A short briefing on the gateway that sits between apps and the FHIR server. It checks who is signing in, decides which records each user can reach, and can be extended with plugins. It works with any FHIR server. Leads: Co-facilitator; supports: Lab facilitator.
    • 13:55–14:05 · Talk · Talk 3: Workflows, sync and who sees what (10 min). A short briefing before Lab 2. A plan definition says what should happen and when, such as the next vaccine due. Sync moves records between the device and the server. The gateway decides which records each user can reach. Leads: Co-facilitator; supports: Lab facilitator.
    • 14:05–14:50 · Lab · Lab 2: Sync data and run a workflow (45 min). In the Player, store records on the device and sync them to the server. Apply an immunization plan definition and see what comes due. Then sign in as two different users through the gateway and compare what each can see, for example 50 records against 10. Leads: Lab facilitator; supports: Co-facilitator.

    Where it meets the journey

    • J2b: Everything Vilay records stays queued on the phone, for up to 28 days, and syncs when a signal returns.
    • J3b：Onesa 接种计划（PlanDefinition）对每一剂次进行评估；在 10 周龄时，6 周龄剂次显示为逾期。
    • J4.0b: The family's consent is recorded before the portal issues the link; the gateway enforces passcode, expiry and consent on every request.
    • J4.1：每次查看都在 Onesa 记录为 AuditEvent，包括通行码错误的尝试。

    结束时，参与者将能够

    • Sync records between the Player and the FHIR server
    • Read a plan definition and follow it through a workflow
    • Show that the gateway filters records by user
