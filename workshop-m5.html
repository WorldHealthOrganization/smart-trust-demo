    Questions for the table

    • J1Does your law let a facility issue a health credential to a child who is not a citizen and has no national ID?
    • J2bWho may create a health identity for a newborn at a home visit, and on whose consent?
    • J4.0aWho may hold and share a child’s record: either parent, a guardian, the child from what age? What does your law say about dependants?
    • J4.0bWhat legal basis lets a record held at home be opened by a clinician abroad, on the family’s consent?
    • J4.0bWould one clause in your procurement — conformance to the national IG — be enough for your portal to issue this link?
    • J4.0bWho sets how a shared link is protected — the passcode, how long it lasts, who may open it: a national rule, or the family’s choice?
    • J4.1What agreement makes every view abroad leave an entry in the home country’s log — and who reads it?
