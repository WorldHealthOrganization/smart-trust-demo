    Technical track Draft

    Two days, one cross-border immunization story. Day 1 covers the building blocks and gets everyone running on Heropa. Day 2 is teams building one part of the journey, then seeing it all work together. Draft for the team to confirm. Names and times are proposals.

    Day 1 · 30 November · Concepts and the OHS stack. By the end of the day you have captured data, synced it, run a workflow and started a dashboard, all on Heropa.
