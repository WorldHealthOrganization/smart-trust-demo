    This page is part of the SMART Trust demonstrator: journeys J1 to J4 (v0.1.0: ci-build) based on FHIR (HL7® FHIR® Standard) v4.0.1. This is the continuous integration build: it follows the source and is not a published version. For a full list of available versions, see the Directory of published versions.

    The DPI-H for Health workshop runs two tracks over two days, both taught from the same journey of one cross-border family. Each policy module asks one question of the journey and fills one sheet of the Country Canvas. Each technical module is paired with the policy module of the same number. Switch tracks on any module page.

    Policy track

    Day 1 · 30 November

    Module Sheet The question it carries Journey steps
    M1 From journey to capabilities Sheet 1 What had to exist for this moment to work — and where does our version of the journey break? J1, J3a
    M2 The reference architecture and where you stand Sheet 2 Which system do we use today for each block — and is it there, partly there, or absent? J2b
    M3 Standards, specifications and the core data set Sheet 3 Which standards do we adopt rather than author — and who owns the national core data set? J2a, J3b

    Day 2 · 1 December

    Module Sheet The question it carries Journey steps
    M4 Trust, credentials and wallets Sheet 4 Who signs, who verifies, and who would hold the national trust anchor? Name it; do not decide it. J1, J2a, J3a, J4.0a
    M5 Governance, decision rights and the legal basis Sheet 4 Who holds each decision — and what legal basis lets the record cross a border and be shared for a child? J1, J2b, J4.0a, J4.0b, J4.1
    M6 Doing it together Sheet 5 Would your neighbour need this too? If yes, why would you each build it? J3b, J4.0b, J4.1
    M7 First move and the investment required Sheet 6 What is realistic in twelve months with the people and money we have — and who owns it? J4.1
