    Module T1 · day 1 · 40 min · Verifiable immunization records across borders: the technical track

    Building with FHIR and the Open Health Stack

    Which FHIR resources, terminologies and tools carry this journey, and where does each lab use them?

    Day 1. Serves the policy module M1 From journey to capabilities.

    Sessions

    • 11:00–11:40 · Talk · Talk 1: Building with FHIR and the Open Health Stack (40 min). A developer's tour of FHIR, then of the tools around it: the HAPI FHIR server, other open global goods, and where the OHS libraries and the OHS Player fit. Leads: Lead facilitator; supports: Co-facilitator.

    Where it meets the journey

    • J1: Patient and Immunization in the hospital record; each dose signed as a DVC (HCERT).
    • J3b: ConceptMap from Taifafeki's product codes to Onesa's schedule codes; the schedule as a PlanDefinition.
    • J4.0b: The IPS as a FHIR document Bundle assembled from the national record.

    À la fin, un participant peut

    • Name the FHIR resources you will use over the two days and say what each is for
    • Explain how terminologies and implementation guides keep data meaningful
    • Say which parts of the stack each lab uses

    Les questions, au fil du parcours

    1. J1
      Un bébé d’Onesa naît à Taifafeki

      Which FHIR resources does the hospital write for a birth dose, and which of their fields end up in the signed card?

    2. J3b
      Les vaccins de Taifafeki sont intégrés au calendrier d’Onesa

      How does an implementation guide let two countries keep their own vaccine codes and still count each other's doses?

    3. J4.0b
      Papa crée un lien à partager à la clinique

      What makes the IPS readable in another country's system without a bilateral agreement?
