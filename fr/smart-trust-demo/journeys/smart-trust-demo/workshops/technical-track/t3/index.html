    Module T3 · day 1 · 15 min · Verifiable immunization records across borders: the technical track

    Trust between countries: SMART Trust and the GDHCN

    When: Day 1 · 13:30–13:45 · Technical track · The two days

    How can a clinic in one country trust a vaccination record from another, and when is a card better than a link?

    Day 1. Serves the policy module M4 Trust, credentials and wallets.

    Sessions

    • 13:30–13:45 · Talk · Talk 2: Trust between countries, SMART Trust and the GDHCN (15 min). How a clinic in one country can trust a vaccination record from another. Covers the GDHCN network, digital vaccine certificates (DVC) and verifiable health links (VHL), and when to use a card and when to use a link. Leads: Trust facilitator.

    Where it meets the journey

    • J1 : La clé de signataire de documents de Taifafeki (DSC de XXR) signe chaque DVC sous forme de CWT dans COSE.
    • J3a: The clinic verifies each card offline against its cached copy of the GDHCN trust list, by kid.
    • J4.1 : Le visualiseur vérifie le HCERT du VHL par rapport au DSC du pays Onesa (XXU) avant de récupérer quoi que ce soit.

    À la fin, un participant peut

    • Explain what the GDHCN is and why a verifier needs a trust list
    • Tell a DVC from a VHL and say when each one fits
    • Know which parts you will work with on Day 2

    Les questions, au fil du parcours

    1. J1
      Un bébé du pays Onesa naît à Taifafeki

      What does the DVC carry, and what does it deliberately leave out, so a card is enough without an identity document?

    2. J3a
      La clinique confirme que les cartes sont authentiques

      How fresh does the cached trust list have to be, and what should the app show when a signature fails?

    3. J4.1
      Un médecin à Taifafeki voit l’historique complet

      Why is the health data not in the QR, and what does the viewer check before it opens the link?
